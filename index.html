FAQ

(10)

Questions, answered.

Short answers to what clients ask before they book.

01BookingHow do I get a price?

Open the quote page, pick the ride and add your addresses. The live map shows your price with tolls included. Send it to us and we confirm by email.

02BookingIs the price final?

It comes from the real driving route, with tolls counted each way. Once we confirm it in writing, that is the price you pay.

03AirportWhere do we meet at MCO?

We confirm the exact meeting point with your booking. Add your flight number and we follow your arrival.

04AirportWhat if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight number you give us and adjust the pickup to your real arrival. Waiting time is set out in your written confirmation.

05The rideHow many people and bags fit?

Up to six passengers with luggage. For a larger group, write to us before booking.

06The rideCan I book by the hour?

Yes. Charter, as directed, starts at three hours, with as many stops as you like inside that time.

07PaymentHow do I pay?

Payment details come with your written confirmation. You receive an invoice from MTC Cargo Corp d/b/a Connexxions Elite.

08PaymentWhat is the cancellation policy?

The cancellation terms come in writing with your confirmation, before you pay anything.

09TripsDo you go beyond Orlando?

Yes. Tampa, the Space Coast, Daytona and other Florida trips are quoted per ride. For longer trips, write to us and we quote it.

10TripsIs there a late-night fee?

Pickups between 10 pm and 5 am add a small late-night fee, shown in your quote before you book.
