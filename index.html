01How accurate is the instant estimate?

It is calculated from typical road distance between your two points. Your official quote locks a flat rate in writing, with tolls and parking included.

02What if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight number you give us and adjust pickup to your real arrival. Free waiting is 60 minutes for international arrivals and 45 for domestic.

03How many people and bags fit?

Six people with reasonable luggage. For seven or more, we arrange a second vehicle with a trusted operator.

04How do I pay?

Credit card or Zelle, charged on confirmation. You receive an invoice from MTC Cargo Corp d/b/a Connexxions Elite.

05What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. Later cancellations are charged 50% of the rate.

06Do you go beyond Orlando?

Yes. Tampa, Miami, Fort Lauderdale and Palm Beach are quoted per trip. Type the address in the planner and request an official quote.
